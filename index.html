How to play

Help every dino find a home!

One dino per row & columnJust like sudoku — no two dinos share a line.
One dino per colourEach colour region gets exactly one dino.
Dinos never touchNot even diagonally — they need personal space.
Tap to mark, tap again to placeCycle: tap for X → dino → clear. Choose Dino or X mark for direct taps. Drag to mark or erase Xs. Auto X is optional and starts off. Keyboard: arrows to move, D for dino, X for a note, Delete to clear.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved with logic alone — no guessing needed.
